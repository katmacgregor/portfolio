Beats 360 is the first Beats headphone with interchangeable ear and headband cushions.

This feature lets users choose between different cushion materials for fit and comfort.

The headphone identifies the attached cushion and adjusts its acoustic tuning to match.

My team led this experience, coordinating the product design, electrical engineering, acoustics, human factors, and firmware teams.
